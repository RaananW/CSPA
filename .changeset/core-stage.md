Add the `stage` material: `setMaterial(id, { kind: "stage", scene })` draws a Strata stage scene on a decorative layer, a lit backdrop and up to 256 particles moved by scene updates with no recompile. The `objects` material now also takes version 3 scenes, whose environment may be a 32 × 16 brightness map instead of the studio.
